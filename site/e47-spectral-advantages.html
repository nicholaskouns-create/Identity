Structural advantages of the E47 invariant kernel

A 47-dimensional invariant code space within the 125-dimensional three-spin carrier, selected by the total Casimir.

H = V₂ ⊗ V₂ ⊗ V₂   ·   K = (C − 6I)(C − 30I)   ·   E₄₇ = ker K

01 · Exact spectral embedding

The SU(2) decomposition is V₀ ⊕ 3V₁ ⊕ 5V₂ ⊕ 4V₃ ⊕ 3V₄ ⊕ 2V₅ ⊕ V₆. The retained spin-2 and spin-5 sectors have dimensions 25 and 22. Hence rank(P₄₇) = Tr(P₄₇) = 47; P₄₇ is a self-adjoint idempotent and commutes with collective SU(2).

02 · Deterministic filtering

The nonzero spectrum of K² is {11664, 12544, 19600, 32400, 186624}. The minimax step is ε = 1/99144, yielding an exact operator-norm residual:

|| (I − K²/99144)ⁿ − P₄₇ ||₂ = (15/17)ⁿ

At n=220, residual ≈ 1.1×10⁻¹².

03 · Noiseless logical factors

E₄₇ ≅ (V₂ ⊗ ℂ⁵) ⊕ (V₅ ⊗ ℂ²). Under collective SU(2), each sector transforms as Dⱼ(U) ⊗ I on its multiplicity factor. Thus ℂ⁵ and ℂ² are noiseless logical subsystems for that noise algebra, including one protected logical qubit in ℂ².
