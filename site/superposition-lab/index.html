A Coin That Has Not Landed

BLUE + GOLD

A qubit can be in a superposition of two basis states.

See the difference

Classical: A hidden state is already blue or gold.

Quantum: The state has amplitudes for blue and gold. Measurement returns one outcome.

The spinning coin is an analogy, not a physical quantum measurement.
