THE GAP THIS CLOSES
-------------------
Before this certificate, the number 47 rested on floating-point eigenvalue
clustering with a 1e-7 tolerance (simulation/validate_e47.py).  The exact int64
certificate (validation/e47_tensor_bracket_trace_exact.py) verifies the Lie
brackets and two traces but computes no rank and no kernel, so it does not
establish 47.  Here dim ker K = 47 is a two-sided exact result from
fraction-free elimination over Z[sqrt 6] -- no tolerance, no eigensolver, no
floating point anywhere in the program.

It is then obtained a second time, independently, with no matrix at all, by
decomposing the SU(2) character chi_2^3 into irreducibles in integer arithmetic.
Two routes with no common step produce the same 47.
