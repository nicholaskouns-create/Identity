First-principles proof

Enumerating tensor weights gives spin multiplicities 1,3,5,4,3,2,1. Enumerating unordered triples gives symmetric spins 0,2,3,4,6, each once. Thus dim ker K=47 and dim ker A=5, since both summands of A are positive semidefinite and commute. On joint sectors A has eigenvalue ((j(j+1)−6)(j(j+1)−30))²+11664(1−s), with s the symmetry eigenvalue. Its spectrum with multiplicities is (0:5,11664:49,19600:9,23328:21,24208:9,31264:18,32400:1,186624:13). The nonzero extrema give optimal constant step 2/(11664+186624)=1/99144 and exact contraction 15/17. The spectral theorem gives Γⁿ=P+(Γ−P)ⁿ for n≥1, hence convergence and fixed point persistence. The basin is R+ker P; the quotient H/ker P is isomorphic to im P. The observable corner is M₅(C); spin addition gives adjoint spins 0 through 4 once each. Schur's lemma gives scalar commutant and center. The density limit for Tr(Pρ)>0 is PρP/Tr(Pρ), with entropy increments tending to zero. External D preserves every basin iff PD restricted to im P is identity. For a particular R, ejection iff PDR≠R; ejection need not delete identity.
