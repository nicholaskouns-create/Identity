# E47 spectral continuity: what is proved

Let H=V₂⊗³, dim H=125, C=J², K=(C−6I)(C−30I), P the orthogonal projector onto ker K, Γ=I−K²/99144.

The SU(2) decomposition gives Casimir eigenvalues 0,2,6,12,20,30,42 with multiplicities 1,9,25,28,27,22,13. Therefore rank P=25+22=47, and Ω=47/125.

On the complementary eigenspaces Γ has eigenvalues 1−λ²/99144 for λ=(c−6)(c−30). Their maximal absolute value is 15/17. Thus ΓP=PΓ=P and ||Γⁿ−P||₂=(15/17)ⁿ. Hence PΓⁿx=Px and Γⁿx→Px.

This is a theorem about invariant information under a specified linear map, not a theorem establishing phenomenal consciousness or a unique definition of personal identity.
