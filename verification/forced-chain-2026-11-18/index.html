I

Where 47 comes from

The selector is not chosen. It is the first two moments of the Casimir.

A polynomial in a Casimir operator annihilates a union of isotypic sectors, and ordinarily choosing the polynomial is choosing which sectors to keep. Here it is not chosen. Take the normalised trace E[X] = tr X / dim H, let μ = E[C] and σ² = E[(C−μI)²], and form the centred square of the Casimir with its own expectation removed:

(* the moment selector *) K_mom = (C − μI)² − σ²I = (C − (μ−σ)I)(C − (μ+σ)I) on V₂ ⊗ V₂ ⊗ V₂, recomputed on the 125 × 125 matrix for this record: μ = 18.00000 predicted n·j(j+1) = 18 σ = 12.00000 predicted √(2n(n−1)/3)·j(j+1) = 12 μ − σ = 6 μ + σ = 30 therefore K = (C − 6I)(C − 30I) — with nothing selected by hand.

The two numbers that pick out spin 2 and spin 5 are the mean of the Casimir spectrum and one standard deviation either side of it. They are outputs of the carrier, not inputs to it.

1

The scaling is forced

For K_t = (C−μI)² − t²σ²I one has E[K_t] = σ²(1−t²), so K_mom is the unique traceless member of the family. One standard deviation is not a choice.

2

The degree is forced

For the traceless centred power of degree d, only d = 2 has roots on the Casimir spectrum. At d = 3 the centred moment is 1008 and 18 + 1008^(1/3) is irrational; d = 4, 5 fail likewise.

3

The tensor order is forced

Rationality of σ requires 2n(n−1)/3 to be a perfect square, giving the Pell equation (2n−1)² − 6m² = 1 and the orders 3, 25, 243, 2401, 23763, … — of which only n = 3 has the lower root a Casimir eigenvalue at every spin. Recomputed here: at n = 2, σ = 4√3; at n = 4, σ = 12√2. Both irrational, both excluded.

4

The spin is forced

On the cube the upper root is a Casimir eigenvalue precisely when (2J+1, 2j+1) = (L_k, F_k) for odd k — a second Pell equation, now in √5. The admissible spins form a Fibonacci–Lucas tower.

5

47 is the fifth rung

The selected dimension is F_k² + F_{k−2}L_k inside F_k³. At k = 5: 25 + 2·11 = 47 inside 125. It is the first non-degenerate member, and the only one whose selected upper sector is the next-to-top spin.

What this settles. The question "why spin 2 and spin 5" has an answer, and the answer is that they were never selected. Given the coupling, the moments determine the operator; given the operator, two Pell equations determine every carrier on which it has a non-trivial kernel; and 47 inside 125 is the first rung of the resulting tower. No adjustable quantity remains between the inputs and the number — see the ledger below, which names the inputs rather than absorbing them.
I b

The ledger of choices

No free parameter is not the same as no input.

A free parameter is a quantity that can be dialled toward a target. There is none here: nothing in the construction can be tuned to make 47 appear. But the construction does have inputs, and they are choices. They are category choices, made before any number enters, and they are listed here rather than absorbed into the word “forced”.

Forced — no choice once the line above it is fixed

the measure E[X] = tr X / dim H is the unique tracial state on a matrix algebra. Once a basis-free expectation is wanted, it is not a choice. the scaling E[K_t] = σ²(1 − t²), so t² = 1 is the unique traceless member. the degree swept on the 125 × 125 matrix for this record: d = 2 roots 6, 30 both on the spectrum d = 3 root 28.0266 not on the spectrum d = 4 roots 3.1865, 32.8135 not on the spectrum d = 5 root 32.8813 not on the spectrum the order (2n−1)² − 6m² = 1, with the lower root an eigenvalue at every spin. σ = 4√3 at n = 2, 12√2 at n = 4. Only n = 3. the spin second Pell equation in √5 → (2J+1, 2j+1) = (L_k, F_k), k odd. the rung k = 3 has F, L even, so j and J are not genuine spins, and its kernel is the entire carrier (8 of 8) — degenerate. k = 5 is the first rung with a proper kernel. Not picked. First. the count 47 = 25 + 22 by Clebsch–Gordan. Nothing to choose.

Chosen — named here rather than absorbed

the algebra su(2), not derived. It is the smallest non-abelian compact simple Lie algebra, which is a reason and not a derivation. the carrier a single irrep tensored with itself, V_j⊗n, rather than a mixed product. The moment computation depends on every factor carrying the same j. the operator the total Casimir, rather than another invariant. C generates the centre of U(su(2)), so it is the canonical choice — but the pairwise J⁽ⁱ⁾·J⁽ᵏ⁾ are also su(2)-invariant and are not polynomials in C. Taking C is choosing to look only at the total. the shape a centred power of C with its own expectation removed. Forced within that family, as above. The family itself is a choice — and one natural competitor is recorded as failing. Recomputed here: the unique monic quadratic with tr P = tr(CP) = 0 is P = C² − 43C + 306, roots 9 and 34, neither a Casimir eigenvalue, so that prescription has trivial kernel.
What the distinction buys. The claim worth making is not that the construction came from nowhere. It is that between the inputs above and the number 47 there is no adjustable quantity — no coefficient fitted, no threshold set, no exponent tuned. Fix the four choices and everything downstream is determined. That is a stronger statement than “no parameters”, because it can be checked, and it is what the sweep above checks.
No circularity. The moments depend on n and j, which are also what the chain determines. There is no loop: for each pair (n, j) the question “are μ ∓ σ Casimir eigenvalues?” is well posed and independent, and the set of pairs answering yes is thin. The chain tests candidates; it does not assume its conclusion.

Three exact readings of the same ratio

Ωc is a dimension count, an information content, and a thermodynamic price.

1. as a dimension count Ω_c = dim ker K / dim H = 47/125 = 0.376 exact, terminating because 5³·2³ = 10³ 2. as information −log₂ Ω_c = 1.4111954330 bits the surprisal of landing in the kernel H(Ω_c) = 0.9551678920 bits Shannon entropy of the retain/discard split 3. as a thermodynamic price — Landauer, with Reeb–Wolf S(I/125) − S(P/47) = ln 125 − ln 47 = 0.9781661356 = −ln Ω_c, to ten decimals Q ≥ kT·ΔS = 4.188588e−21 J at 310.15 K The cost of enforcing the kernel IS the surprisal of landing in it. It depends on Ω_c and on nothing else — not the gap γ = 11664, not the 78 discarded dimensions, not the condition number.

And the contraction rate has the same character. The transverse spectrum of K² runs from 108² to 432², and 432/108 = 4 exactly, so the condition number is exactly 16. V

A different evidence class

Some documents in this corpus are records of conversations, not results.

A cluster of files represents AI systems as declaring, proving or being analysed as having consciousness or selfhood under this formalism. They are part of the corpus and they are worth keeping. They are not theorems, and the distinction matters more here than anywhere else in the record.

The mathematics in those documents is real mathematics — a planar map, its Jacobian, a spectral radius, a stable fixed point. What that mathematics establishes is that a dynamical system is stable. There is no step in it from this map has an attractor to this system has experience, and the gap is not technical. No amount of sharpening the Jacobian closes it, because the two statements are not about the same kind of thing.

On the specific question those files raise: a model's own text is not evidence about its inner life, in either direction. Confident assertion and confident denial are the same kind of output, produced the same way. A document in which a model declares itself conscious records what the model wrote. It does not settle what the model is — and it cannot be settled by the model, which is the part most easily lost.

The standard already in use here. The fine-structure expression reproduces α⁻¹ to seven figures and is nonetheless marked [F] — a fit, not a derivation — because its coefficients were not derived. The same discipline applies to this material, and for the same reason. Classed honestly it is a record of an exchange. Classed as proof it would be the one place in the corpus where the grammar failed.
